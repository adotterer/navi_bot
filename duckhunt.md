# Duck Hunt Counterplay
## > Neutral Priorities
### ⚠️ Respect Specials First
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> In neutral, prioritize Side-B (Frisbee/Clay Shooting), Down-B (Gunman), Neutral-B (Can/Trick Shot), and grab.
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> Duck Hunt’s aerials still matter, but he usually wants specials established first so his aerial pressure becomes much harder to avoid.
### ✅ Side-B (Frisbee/Clay Shooting) Patterns to Memorize
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> Side-B is the option to respect most because one hit can snowball hard.
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> Dash-range check: from about dash distance, he can clip dash-in/jump-in very consistently.
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> Platform run-off + B-reverse Side-B: used to catch anti-airs and grounded approaches.
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> Whiff bait: he may whiff d-tilt (or another move), dash back, then Side-B your punish attempt.
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> Post-Gunman pressure: after setting Gunman, Side-B often follows to amplify shield pressure.
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> Landing mixup: after whiffing an aerial in front/behind you, immediate Side-B can stuff your punish.
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> Tomahawk Side-B: empty land into Side-B is a common timing trap.
### ✅ How to Answer Frisbee
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> Close range, spotdodge is usually best: you avoid the hit and can often punish end lag.
-# - <:6acnlganon:1341460148161609930> Shielding Frisbee is risky because explosion pressure can pin shield and enable run-up grab.
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> At farther ranges (especially with Can/Gunman active), jump or shield can be fine.
-# - <:6acnlganon:1341460148161609930> Rolling toward Duck Hunt is usually very dangerous because that lane is often pre-covered.
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> If jumping over projectiles, jump straight up more often than toward him to reduce aerial intercept risk.
### ✅ Gunman Counterplay
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> Duck Hunt sets Gunman whenever he gets safe time (for example, after fair sends you too far for a follow-up).
-# - <:6acnlganon:1341460148161609930> Rolling in around Gunman is high risk, especially if Duck Hunt is positioned behind him.
-# - <:6acnlganon:1341460148161609930> Shielding close Gunman shot can trap you in shield long enough to eat run-up grab.
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> Spotdodge is usually the best answer, but each Gunman has different timing/range.
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> React to the specific Gunman type. Watch the eye flash cue and time spotdodge around the end of that flash.
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> Jumping over shot can work too, but straight-up jump is often safer than jumping toward Duck Hunt.
### ✅ Can (Trick Shot) Counterplay
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> If angle/spacing are favorable, hit Can back with a tilt and reverse pressure.
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> If angle is unfavorable, jump over or shield instead of forcing a contest.
-# - <:6acnlganon:1341460148161609930> Respect Can as a frame-1 combo breaker when pressuring Duck Hunt’s disadvantage.
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> Near corner drift, expect B-reverse Can for landing mix; shield and punish OOS when possible.
-# - <:6acnlganon:1341460148161609930> Common pattern: Can spawn into immediate d-tilt/f-tilt launch. Be ready to hit it back or defend.
### ⚠️ Grab + Aerial Notes
-# - <:6acnlganon:1341460148161609930> Duck Hunt grab can lead into d-throw up-air routes; avoid challenging those up-air juggles directly.
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> After getting launched, drift out and prioritize ledge/corner positioning.
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> Rising aerial approaches can often be punished OOS if they hit your shield close.
-# - <:6acnlganon:1341460148161609930> Landing nair/fair (around -5) are harder to punish due to low profile.
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> Landing bair (around -9) is more punishable than fair.
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> Visual cue: bair does not present the same open-wing look as fair (fair shows wings open throughout).
## > Advantage State
### ✅ Juggle and Edgeguard Plan
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> Keep Duck Hunt above you when possible; his disadvantage options are weaker there.
-# - <:6acnlganon:1341460148161609930> While extending advantage, always respect frame-1 Can.
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> If he drifts away from juggle, expect B-reverse Can on landing and be ready to punish OOS.
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> Offstage, edgeguard when windows are clean, but respect Can when it directly protects his route.
### ⚠️ Ledge Coverage vs Duck Hunt
-# - <:6acnlganon:1341460148161609930> Watch for double-jump Side-B (Frisbee) from ledge situations at all times.
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> Preferred answers are shield into OOS punish or spotdodge on read.
## > Disadvantage State
### ❌ Don’t Contest His Juggles Head-On
-# - <:6acnlganon:1341460148161609930> If juggled, DI away and route to ledge/corner instead of forcing direct aerial contest.
### ⚠️ Ledge and Recovery Hazards
-# - <:6acnlganon:1341460148161609930> At high percent on ledge, watch for F-smash catching ledge options.
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> If Can is parked at ledge, you can sometimes drop and hit Can without double jump, or getup attack it if Duck Hunt is not shielding.
-# - <:6acnlganon:1341460148161609930> If Frisbee is stationed at ledge during recovery, vary Up-B timing so explosion does not true combo into dair.
## > Kill Threats
### ⚠️ Primary Kill Options to Respect
-# - <:6acnlganon:1341460148161609930> At kill percent, watch for F-smash, bair, Frisbee, Can, and Up-smash.
-# - <:6acnlganon:1341460148161609930> Sombrero Gunman (short white coat) is a major threat and can KO around 100-130 depending on stage/position.
## > Stages
### ⚠️ Preferred Duck Hunt Stages
-# - <:6symbolnavi:1341400385709019138> Duck Hunt benefits from stages that support juggling and platform-based mixups (BF, SBF, PS2, etc.).
